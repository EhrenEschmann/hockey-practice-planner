    Tips

    • Drag & drop: drag the Coach (or Skater, Cone, Tire, Puck, Net) button straight onto the ice to drop one there.
    • Small cones: drag to lay a row of them. A puck carrier whose path runs along the row stickhandles the puck through them during animation.
    • Goalie: click (or drag the Goalie button) near a net and the goalie is placed in the crease facing out; or select a net and press Add goalie.
    • Coach: a diamond marker with a label. Coaches can receive passes and pass or shoot — use the Puck tool on a coach (or drop a puck on them) and set their Facing. They can move too: use the Skater tool on a coach to give them a path.
    • Starts moving: in a skater's or coach's panel, choose "when #1 reaches…" and a waypoint — they wait until that player gets there (plus their start delay).
    • Offense / Defense: the O / D switch under the Skater tool colours new skaters blue (offense) or red (defense), numbered per side. Change a skater's side (and colour) in its Selection panel.
    • Skater: click to place, keep clicking to add waypoints (or drag to draw freehand). Enter/Esc finishes. Click an existing skater to extend its path.
    • Puck pile: place a pile, then in a skater's panel press Take puck from pile — they pick one up where their path passes the pile (or use Give a puck to… on the pile). Then add passes and shots as usual.
    • Passes & shots: select a puck, add a Pass/Shoot, then Mark on path and click where on the skater's path it happens — or drag the P / S marker along the path. A pass to a moving player can instead be timed by the receiver ("arriving as #2 reaches …") — the R marker shows where they get it. Tick off the boards for a bank pass (drag the B marker along the boards) — a player can bank it to themselves.
    • Barricade / Arrow: click points, double-click or Enter to finish.
    • Raised pad / Jump pad: run a skater's path through a raised pad and they slide under it; across a low jump pad and they jump over it — pushing the puck ahead either way.
    • Zone / Obstacle: drag a box. Use "Focus" on a zone to plan a drill in just that section.
    • Select: drag objects, drag waypoint handles, double-click a handle to remove it. Arrow keys nudge. Delete removes.
    • Wheel to zoom, Space/middle-mouse to pan.
